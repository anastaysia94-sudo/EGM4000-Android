# EGM4000 Safety Boundaries

EGM4000 is a gameplay intelligence and session-feedback project. Its safe launch boundary is strict:

- User-authorized evidence only.
- Local-first by default.
- No guaranteed profit or winnings claims.
- No hidden server-state claims.
- No balance manipulation.
- No bypassing third-party protections.
- No credential storage for Fire Kirin or any other third-party platform.
- No claim that correlations predict future random outcomes.
- F.S.A. remains a separate controlled/sandbox game product when used for exact telemetry validation.

Acceptable feedback examples:

- Your recorded shot pace increased during a drawdown.
- Your logged break reminders were ignored in this session.
- Your replay shows several high-frequency bursts before credit decrease.
- This is a hypothesis worth testing, not a prediction.

Unacceptable feedback examples:

- This will win.
- The next payout is due.
- The server is about to pay.
- EGM4000 can beat Fire Kirin.
- EGM4000 can recover losses.
