Evidence boundary

EGM4000 observes only pixels explicitly shared by the user. It does not intercept credentials, read hidden provider APIs, bypass protections, alter balances, or guarantee random outcomes.

Credit-HUD and weapon-HUD changes can be detected as visual changes. Numeric credits or weapon levels are not asserted unless a future validated numeric/icon extractor reaches sufficient confidence; EGM4000 will not invent values.
