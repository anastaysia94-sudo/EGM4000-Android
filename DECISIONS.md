# DECISIONS

Updated: 2026-09-26

## Standing project decisions
- Separate measured, calculated, inferred, hypothesized, and unknown information.
- Do not claim access to hidden third-party game mechanics or guaranteed prediction.
- EGM/F.S.A. acceptance is limited to owned or explicitly authorized non-cash environments, synthetic replay, and authorized telemetry.
- The cross-repo F.S.A. runtime bridge is an integration contract/evidence path, not permission to use third-party real-money accounts or bypass platform controls.
- Exact-telemetry labels must remain distinct from inference and prediction.
- Another LLM or developer must begin with AI_HANDOFF.md and repository evidence.
- Never commit credentials, tokens, private customer data, or secrets.
- Record what changed, why, verification evidence, remaining blocker, and rollback risk after material work.
